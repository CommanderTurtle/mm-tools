What MiniMax can hear in a structured caption
Global Metadata

Genre and fusion, tempo, key or mode when intentional, emotional progression, listening scene, and the overall sonic profile.

Vocal Details

Lead configuration, register, timbre, delivery, section changes, harmonies, doubles, diction, and restrained vocal effects.

Arrangement

A chronological instrument lifecycle: what enters, exits, intensifies, strips back, transitions, and carries the ending.

Best practice

Use vivid sentences, specify two or three defining elements, preserve hard exclusions, and leave everything else room to breathe.
